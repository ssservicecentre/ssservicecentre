Cancellation and Refund Policy

Notice to Customers:

The specific terms of cancellation, inspection charges (if any), and refund eligibility are subject to the direct confirmation of the business owner at the time of service initiation.

General Guidelines

  • Cancellations: Service requests can typically be cancelled before the technician begins physical diagnosis or orders specific replacement parts.
  • Diagnosis Fees: An inspection or visiting charge may apply depending on the location and effort, which will be communicated prior to the visit.
  • Refunds: Since repair involves labor and specific electronic components, refunds are generally evaluated on a case-by-case basis if a replaced part fails within a stipulated warranty period provided directly by the business.

Please discuss exact terms with our representative via phone or WhatsApp before authorizing any repair work.
